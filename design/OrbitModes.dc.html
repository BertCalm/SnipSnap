SPEED
THE NEEDLE COVERS THE SAME 16THS PER SECOND ON EVERY RING, SO A BIGGER RING TAKES LONGER TO COME ROUND. 16 AGAINST 20 IS 4/4 AGAINST 5/4 — A POLYMETER. A BAR AND A HALF IN, THE OUTER NEEDLE IS STILL ON ITS FIRST LAP.

LAP
