These are rendered audio files from a source-backed experiment. They are here to be judged, not to declare the sound solved.

01

Small, on purpose.

A finite gathering of 1–8 performers. Each performer has a repeatable identity, its own timing and small pitch differences.

02

A voice stays the source.

The candidate uses time-domain pitch-synchronous overlap-add (TD-PSOLA). Listen critically for processing artifacts, repeated consonants and vowel changes.

03

Listen first. Integrate next.

No HOLD mode or full phone integration yet. Every recording includes its render recipe and diagnostics so the result can be inspected.
