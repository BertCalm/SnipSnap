05 / UNDER THE SURFACE

A prototype, with
the seams showing.

These are rendered audio files from a source-backed experiment. They are here to be judged, not to declare the sound solved.

01

The technique is in the recording.

The wordless bank uses four recorded gestures from two singers. Low, breathy, clear and rounded describe the performed sources; the engine arranges their roles and entrances.

02

Small, inspectable transformations.

Performers use conservative time-domain pitch-synchronous overlap-add (TD-PSOLA), repeatable identities and small timing and pitch differences. Listen for artifacts, repeated consonants and vowel changes.

03

A starter kit in the app.

The new build includes wordless rendered pads under CONFLUENCE. Source editing and HOLD are still future work. Every audition recording includes its render recipe and diagnostics.
