Why it is built this way

Keep the decision small.

Your preference for model 1 sets the reference. Luthier’s review focused this experiment on the quiet venue contribution. The direct instruments and event sequence remain sample-identical before the shared playback gain.

The two alternatives use an audition-only return multiplier of 1.8× (+5.1 dB). Their saved production patch describes the unlifted venue endpoint; the separate audition recipe records the multiplier and postmix rule.

Source and comparison method

Exact source snapshots and SHA-256 hashes accompany the clips.

Baseline: original model-1 prelevel bus. Each alternative: original bus − original venue + 1.8 × endpoint venue, with Float rounding recorded in the source. Bass, upper harp, sympathetic stems and strum events are checked unchanged. All three use one playback scalar and a common peak guard.

Energy, upper-mid and tail limits are engineering review guardrails. They do not establish that a variant sounds better. The earlier model-2 comparison remains available in the earlier audition.
