Method and source

Source snapshots and SHA-256 hashes accompany the study.

The source patch is pinned to model 1, MIDI 48, seed 7, CURRENT 0.20. The new venue motion is a separate audition process near 2 Hz, not a remapping of that saved CURRENT control. Fixed delays, absorption filters and nonnegative apertures make feed-forward returns. Nothing feeds back into the harp.

Candidates retain the original full bus, subtract its old venue contribution, and add the new return. The source patch alone reproduces the original; the audition wrapper records the extra return algorithm and common gains. Space-only previews share a separate common gain. The short sequence trims each full mix to two seconds and fades only the preview cuts.

Source invariants and signal checks establish reproducibility and bounds. They do not establish audibility or musical quality. The gentle-return comparison and earlier full audition remain available.
