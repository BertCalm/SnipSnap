# CUTTINGS

Version 1 sample-bank instrument for SnipSnap. The owner accepted Study 04's sampler-colored sound on 2026-11-15. Pocket Finds preserves its fourteen hits and two one-bar phrases exactly; new arrangements and longer phrases still require listening acceptance.

The gesture is **a found attack → a different recording's body or room → a deliberate cut → light early-sampler color**. Sources are original procedural imaginary sessions, not extracted commercial recordings. This lands as a versioned kit family, not another registered drum synthesizer. Printed samples preserve the approved sound; there is no editable source-engine macro panel or claim that a saved recipe re-synthesizes the scenes.

## Shelf

| Starter | Tempo | Arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CUTTINGS POCKET FINDS | 89 BPM | Accepted boom-bap signature cuts, bass catches, dry-to-room snare, jingle hat and reverse-metal offcut |
| CUTTINGS NIGHT FRAGMENTS | 76 BPM | Felt kick and brush backbeat lead; hand percussion, lowered tuning, gentler damping and longer metal shadows |
| CUTTINGS CUT CIRCUIT | 124 BPM | Clap backbeat, alternating kicks, raised snare/rim tuning, short hats, gated cymbal/metal roles |

Every kit has twenty pads: fourteen finite hits followed by one-bar roll/riser, two-bar roll/riser and four-bar roll/riser. Duration is selected by playing a different pad, so it survives playback and export without relying on a live HOLD parameter. Phrases are printed at the kit's saved tempo; there is no live tempo-following promise. Closed and open hats share a choke group.

The genre arrangements change source allocation, backbeat role, tuning, envelopes and groove structure. They are not three strengths of the same effect. A one-bar Pocket phrase remains the exact approved recording; its longer versions are composed from the accepted source hits, as are the Night/Circuit phrases.

## Source and implementation

- `synth/src/main/resources/cuttings/v1/` holds 192 accepted Study 04 mono 44.1 kHz WAVs and a manifest with SHA-256 hashes. The bank is approximately 13.3 MB. Each of sixteen source roles has three distinct strike levels and four alternate performances per level.
- `CuttingsKits` loads owned buffers, arranges the three genres, and composes the longer phrases. Genre transposition uses band-limited cubic sampling; positive pitch changes receive an eighth-order 8.5 kHz prefilter. Pocket's original samples bypass all reprocessing.
- `StarterKits` and `StarterShelf` expose the three fixed kits. `ArrangedPad.velocityTakes` carries the complete grid into the shared save/playback/export path. These are rendered banks with provenance, not synthetic recipes that only reconstruct one anchor hit.
- `scripts/freeze-cuttings-bank.py` freezes only the exact `cuttings-r4-sampler-color-1` version and rejects changes to an already-frozen v1 manifest before copying any samples. The Python renderer and its source seeds remain in `prototype/cuttings/`.

## Reproduce and verify

```sh
./gradlew --no-daemon :synth:test --tests '*CuttingsKitsTest' :shell:test --tests '*CuttingsRoundTripTest'
./gradlew --no-daemon :shell:generateCuttingsKits -PcuttingsOutput=/absolute/output
python3 scripts/package-cuttings-factory.py /absolute/output /absolute/site/dist/factory
```

The generator builds real shelf kits, saves/reloads them, verifies PCM windows against all source grids, checks two take cycles per zone and native MPC 3 metadata, and emits native kit folders, MPC 3, MPC 2 and SFZ exports. Audition WAVs are extracted from the saved kit using `PadHit.resolve`, rather than generated by a separate browser/Python engine. Native kits and exports retain 24-bit PCM. The hosted factory page uses 16-bit listening copies to meet the hosting size limit; packaging checks the conversion error is at most one half of a 16-bit sample step. The Study 04 comparison page retains its existing audio.

`CuttingsKitsTest` verifies asset hashes, exact accepted PCM, distinct genre role orders, dynamic energy, take diversity, numerical bounds, silent endpoints, phrase lengths and hat grouping. `CuttingsRoundTripTest` exercises all 127 velocity values and nine successive hits per pad before and after SFZ export/import, checks saved kit equality, exported PCM and playback windows, and checks both MPC formats' supported metadata. A metadata check is not hardware playback evidence.

## Why it is built this way

- Freezing the accepted performances prevents integration from subtly replacing the sound the owner chose.
- Source-role changes and composed grooves give the genres different musical jobs while retaining the shared collage identity.
- Separate duration pads keep the full roll/riser performance, velocity layers and round robins intact through export.
- Hardware evidence stays separate because correct sample files and metadata alone cannot prove an MPC selects the intended take.
