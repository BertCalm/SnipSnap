# Instrument acceptance gates

Owner requirements, supplied 2026-11-10. Apply the relevant gates to each engine;
a passing sound preview alone does not establish instrument completion.

- **Melodic:** at least six distinct musical voices with presets, playable
  keygroups, a premade kit and chord-tool compatibility.
- **Articulation:** appropriate HOLD. Picked/thumbed string voices use STRUM,
  with the intended-character exception for distinctive repeats.
- **Percussion:** authored roll/riser where appropriate; individual voices may
  remain finite.
- **Drums:** multiple distinct genre kits on the shelf.
- **All instruments:** working velocity levels and round robin through
  rendering, saving, playback and export.

Record the evidence for each stage and target. Check timbral velocity changes
and take diversity, rather than counting differently named copies. Save/load
and export/import checks must exercise selection and playback windows as well
as metadata. Do not promote a single-layer fallback or a dropped take to a pass.
An SFZ round trip does not prove MPC behavior; format and hardware limitations
remain open gates until the relevant evidence exists. Mark genuinely inapplicable
melodic or articulation requirements as such instead of inventing controls.
