# PRISM spectral-motion audition

This finite-note prototype adapts XOrbital's A/B spectral profiles to PRISM's
existing 32-partial offline renderer. The carrier frequencies, note,
articulation and deterministic phase seed stay with spectrum A. Spectrum B
changes partial amplitudes. Both spectra pass through PRISM's existing
spectral controls, root protection and fixed headroom calculation.

`PrismPatch.motion` is optional. An absent field preserves historical JSON and
the original renderer. A saved `PrismMotion` contains all 32 destination
amplitudes, the starting blend (`morph`), the travel amount (`evolve`), the
transition duration (`seconds`) and progressively delayed upper groups
(`groupLag`). The easing is a smoothstep. No effect or time-varying output
normalization is added.

## Listen and reproduce

```text
./gradlew :synth:test --tests 'com.snipsnap.synth.Prism*'
./gradlew :synth:generatePrismMotionAudition
```

The generator writes 31 cases under `testkit/prism-motion-audition/`, each with
native and matched PCM16 WAVs plus a complete `PadRecipe` JSON. It verifies
exact recipe replay. Native files are normal conditioned engine output before
extra audition matching; they are not the internal additive sum. Matched files
use the shared loudest-200-ms RMS target of .03 with a peak guard.

The four families are metallic strike to vowel, organ to glass, pluck to reed,
and a synthesized-donor imprint. Each has the original A, static B, .25/.8/1.8
second transitions and a staggered-group variant. The organ also has low and
high register comparisons. Start with matched A, medium motion and B; use the
native version to judge the engine's level behavior.

The fourth family's donor is generated by SnipSnap's VOX ROBOT engine. Its
source audio and true VOX recipe are included. `PrismImprint.endpoint` reads
the donor through `Transplant.bandLevels`, then samples that smoothed,
fixed-frequency colour at the PRISM carriers. The 1024-point analyzer does not
provide accurate low-register harmonic extraction or reconstruct the donor.
The measured coefficients are saved, so playback needs no donor recording.

The manifest records source revision, dirty state, source hashes, file hashes,
recipe replay results and floating-point level/spectral/pitch measurements.
Local pitch and centroid estimates support listening; they do not establish
sonic approval. The browser stores notes locally and exports them as JSON.

During implementation, an independent copy of the original renderer from
`3c0d1c781b803d53745bf38032198f78c1f918fa` produced sample-identical output
for all six default voices in both finite and held modes. The 12 reference
hashes and their runtime are recorded in
`synth/src/test/resources/audition/prism-motion-original-sha256.tsv`.

## Current boundaries

This is a finite mono audition, with no new phone controls or factory presets.
Motion patches explicitly reject HOLD's loop region. A sustained cyclic
trajectory and keygroup integration need their own implementation and listening
checks. Existing PRISM held notes retain the original renderer.

Endpoint helpers evaluate broad vowel/reed/glass shapes at the requested
carrier frequencies. Saved endpoints are coefficient arrays: subsequently
changing TUNE transposes their colour with the note. Recompute an endpoint to
retain a fixed-Hz envelope at a different root.

The first decision is whether this motion adds a useful audible character to
PRISM. The roster remains a listening experiment pending the owner's verdict.

The owner's first verdict heard the movement but did not favor the underlying
source sounds. The [source-palette round](PRISM_PALETTE.md) auditions eight
broader candidates as original source, static imprint and moving imprint.
