# UX pass, November 2026: the pad screen for a new user

*Started 2026-11-15. The owner's words: the pad screen is "too complicated to
understand and to tell what you can do, what things are, where they are, etc.
Pretend a dumb person is going to use the app." It builds on the
[UI clarity spec](superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-ui-clarity-design.md) and the
[November UAT](UAT_2026_11.md). Where they disagree, the owner's answers below
win.*

## The owner's answers (2026-11-15)

The audit, the mockups and the forms were a listening-page-style artifact
("Simpler Pads"). The owner answered:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Which screen | All of it: from opening the app to changing a pad's sound |
| Extra features (Aquarium, pedals, effects) | Simple first, more later: a few plain choices, the rest behind labelled doors; nothing deleted |
| The shared fixes (a to g, below) | All of them |
| "Change the sound" | **B, pick what you want**: effects grouped by what they do, each with a one-line description |
| Unclear effect names (DUB, FORGE, BODY, SMEAR…) | Rename them in plain words; a rename list goes to the owner first |
| Open the app on the pads | Yes, when a kit is open |
| Aquarium | Open; asked how it differs from pedals (answered: a pedal is one effect with one knob; the Aquarium is six effects already wired together, with presets and 52 controls, and is the only effect that also runs live on the whole kit) |

## What a new user met (the audit, at `70a2b1f1c`)

- The pads are under PROJECT ▸ PADS; the app opens on LIBRARY.
- An echo on a kick took four taps (tap, OPEN ▸, EFFECTS ▸, open the box also
  called EFFECTS) and a search through 39 one-word chips with no description,
  plus 12 more pedals behind MORE.
- EFFECTS named a button, a page and a box. There were two Aquariums (a live
  kit bus and a per-pad print), neither described in words.
- One idea under many names (echo: ECHO, DELAY, ON BEAT, a SLAP slider), and
  one name for several ideas (VARIATIONS; SUSTAIN).
- Tapping an empty pad said only "Long-press to capture."; the treated corner
  had no key; RAW was not explained; Help said nothing about effects.

Opening on the pads is built (PR 4). The app remembers the project that is
open while one of its kits is on the grid (`shell/.../LastOpen.kt`, the
preference `last-open-project`), and a cold start opens that project on PADS
through `openProject`, the same landing its row gives. It does so once per
start, after the kit list is read, and only from where a cold start lands: the
library with nothing open, no share waiting and nothing busy. Closing the
project, or a project with no kit, forgets it, so the next start is the
library as before; so does a project whose active kit has left the shelf.

## Direction B

Built in PR 3. The pad's EFFECTS box now opens on a list sorted by what you
want: START HERE (eight drum-safe starters), nine closed groups whose closed
line names what is inside, then EFFECT CHAIN and SHAPE. Each row is a name,
one line saying what it does, and ON with its AMT when the pad carries it; a
tap takes the same door the old chip, pedal line, board or box takes. The old
chip rows, the pedal row, MORE and COPY/PASTE sit in a closed ALL AS BUTTONS
section below the list: nothing was deleted. The data is
`shell/.../EffectsMenu.kt`; the list is `app/.../EffectsList.kt`.

### The owner's decisions (2026-11-15)

- The rename list in full (`docs/VOCABULARY.md`, E2), with DRONE becoming
  ENDLESS, not FREEZE: KIT TOOLS already has a FREEZE.
- The names land with this list, not on the old six-wide chips, which fit
  about six letters.
- After the Luthier check: CUT NOISE is CUT TAIL (HUSH shortens a hit; it
  does not clean noise), WIDE SPACE is DREAMY SPACE (VELOUR adds no width),
  HISS is OLD RECORDING (DUST lays a real recording's hiss and room under the
  pad).
- Nine groups: HIT HARDER, DIRTY OR OLD, ECHO AND SPACE, MOVEMENT, FILTER AND
  WAH, IN TIME WITH THE BEAT, PITCH AND REVERSE, SOFTER OR LONGER, STRANGE.
  DRIVE moved to DIRTY OR OLD, CUT TAIL to HIT HARDER, BEAT ECHO to IN TIME,
  RISING ECHOES and THROUGH SPEAKER to STRANGE, and the four filters and wahs
  have their own group.
- A START HERE set for drums: PUNCH, HARD CLIP, SHARPER HIT, ROOM REVERB,
  QUICK ECHO, CRUSH, SUB, REVERSE.

Decisions this PR made where the owner's list was silent, for the owner to
overrule on the phone: the RE-AMP AND ROOM box is SPEAKER AND ROOM (its moves
are THROUGH SPEAKER and REAL ROOM, and the box's old name would not fit the
COMBINE card's 44-character line); the second door is MIX WITH ANOTHER SOUND ▸
with the back name MIX, and its closed line now says what is inside (LAYER,
JOIN, BLEND, BORROW TONE AND MORE) instead of repeating the door; KIT TOOLS'
DUST ALL keeps its name.

### Luthier ledger

- **Verified:** every name and line was checked against the code at
  `fec4631ef` (Treatments, Shuffle, Pedals, Keyed, the section files, Eras,
  BoardRecipe, OutsideSheet).
- **Computed, not rendered:** the times in the lines (QUICK ECHO about 80 to
  90 ms, SLOWER about 4 semitones at a tap) come from the macro laws at AMT 0.7.
- **Proposed:** the groups, the order within each, START HERE.
- **Heard:** nothing. Three questions only the owner's ears can settle: does
  BLUR read as slowed; does DREAMY SPACE read as space on a phone; do WIDE,
  THICKEN and SIX PLAYERS sound different from each other on a phone speaker
  (the preview averages the channels, so their width cannot be heard there).
- **Long sounds:** FADE IN, VIBRATO, PITCH DROP, TREMOLO and SPIN SPEAKER are
  for held sounds (the owner's gate notes); ROLL, CHOP IN TIME and PUMP refuse
  a hit shorter than two divisions; IN KEY refuses drums. None of these is in
  START HERE.
