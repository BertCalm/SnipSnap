WHAT YOU ARE LISTENING FOR

AEROSTAT is one imaginary instrument: a strike on two tubes, a rotor that catches a valve, and a shared reservoir that blows a whistle at the same pitch. Exhaust warms a vessel. The vessel rises and settles, and that motion changes the tail. Both banks always play. There is no bank selector.

Listen for the gesture, in order: knock, a short wait, the whistle blooming, the pressure falling, a floating tail. A tube hit followed by an unrelated pad does not pass. The tube tap should be the knock alone. The flow tap should arrive later and be the whistle. Quick is brighter and earlier. Heavy is darker and about five cents sharp. At LIFT 0 the tail should barely move in pitch. At LIFT 1 it may drift, and the note should still be the note.

The held loop is the settled whistle only. The static sample has no place to mark where a loop starts, so the opening strike is not in that buffer. Use REPEAT on the loop card and listen for a click or a pump. A phrase shares one reservoir: the second strike should not be a copy of the first.

Every clip is brought to one loudness after it is rendered, including the raw clip, so a control that only made something quieter will not show up as quiet. It has to show up as a different tone. Mark each KEEP, MEH or CUT. A CUT on a knob end means that end goes too far. Then answer the four questions and press SAVE VERDICT.
