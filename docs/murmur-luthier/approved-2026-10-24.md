# Owner-preferred Murmur candidates

The owner's exported listening notes prefer the candidate in all six comparisons: KEY MOTION 0, .5, .75 and 1 at C4, and CHOIR HOLD at C2 and C3. The complete original export is [listening-2026-10-24.json](listening-2026-10-24.json). Its embedded manifest exactly matches the published comparison, including source and audio hashes. There are no additional written sound notes in the export.

Newly created patches use a new saved render version. KEY's formant target gains are multiplied by three, capped at the existing limit of three. CHOIR HOLD fits one complete near-unison beat period from C2 through C3. The C2/C3 cycle counts and finishing path reproduce the selected experiment exactly. Higher CHOIR notes and finite CHOIR retain their previous synthesis. Custom detunes fall back to the established loop planner if a complete beat period would be too short, too long or invalid.

All existing saved render versions retain their previous arithmetic. The 36 factory patches and historical audition generators explicitly retain the previous saved configurations; the selected defaults do not silently revise their sounds. Missing saved configuration/version fields still decode as the original renderer, and the shared patch schema is unchanged.

## Why it is built this way

- A saved render version makes the selected loop policy reproducible without a special development-only wrapper.
- Stronger KEY targets are stored directly in the patch. Rendering never multiplies saved gains again, so edited and reloaded patches retain their identity.
- The longer loop is limited to the heard low-register range. Applying the same fixed cycle count at high notes would create very short, unreviewed loops.
- Existing presets are complete authored sounds. Keeping their configurations explicit lets new defaults improve without changing old kits or saved work.

## Reproduction and acceptance

```sh
./gradlew --no-daemon :synth:generateMurmurApprovedAudition
```

The output contains six ordinary patch JSON files, six complete pad recipes, raw/matched WAVs, a provenance manifest and a ZIP. Generation rejects any WAV that differs from the corresponding owner-selected publication hash. These saved recipes require a build containing the new renderer; the previously published phone APK remains the earlier build.

Approval covers the six selected comparisons. It does not approve every macro/register corner, revise the factory bank's separate listening status, or establish physical phone/MPC playback. Numerical checks cover the intervening low CHOIR notes and unchanged upper-register/other-role behavior. Validation results are recorded after the coordinated checks.
