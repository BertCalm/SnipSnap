# Loading feedback, PR 2c: jobs the app owns, guards, silent loads

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** The dock knows about every job that is still running, including
one whose screen you have left. Project actions cannot be double-tapped.
The remaining silent loads (kit bank, Keys, Surface, setting the key, Takes
bin) say what they are doing. A button's leftover "working" state stops
announcing work that has already finished.

**Architecture:**

- An app-owned **job board** for work that outlives its screen:
  - `JobBoard` in `:shell` is pure and tested on the JVM.
  - `AppJobs` in `:app` holds Compose state on top of it and is exposed as
    `LocalJobs`.
  - The dock shows `busy ?: jobs.current ?: screenBusy`.
- The pad editor reports every job through the board instead of
  `ReportBusy`, so leaving the editor no longer silences a write that keeps
  running on `appScope`.
- Screens whose work dies with them keep using `ReportBusy`, one call site
  per screen.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-ui-clarity-design.md` §7.6.
Rulings that led here are recorded in the 2a/2b ledger:

- appScope jobs get a job board.
- `setKey`/`inKey` must not write `screenBusy` directly.
- TakesBinScreen joins the guard law.
- A ring that lingers stops announcing "working".

Real counts (Export files, Chop All, Dust All, Breed, starter "n of 16")
are out of scope here. The owner deferred them to a later PR 2d on
2026-10-29.

**Base:** the default branch at `b6248f426`. Branch `claude/loading-c`.

## Review focus

1. A job that starts on the pad editor and keeps running after the user
   leaves (a treatment write) stays in the dock until it ends, and leaves
   no stale entry behind. Task 2's device test pins this.
2. A job that throws or is cancelled ends its board entry. `end()` runs
   in `finally` (Tasks 1 and 2).
3. Two jobs at once: the dock shows the most recent one. When it ends,
   the older one shows again (Task 1, JVM).
4. A double tap on NEW PROJECT creates one project (Task 3, law and
   device check).
5. A button whose ring lingers after the job ended no longer says
   "working" to TalkBack (Task 5).
