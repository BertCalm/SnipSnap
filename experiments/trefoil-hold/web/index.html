Design, measurements & downloads

The finite bank and hardware audition were accepted by the owner on 1 November 2026. The owner approved Section 2’s note-off sound and reported clicks elsewhere. This playback revision adds scheduled repeats and short transport transitions while preserving every recording. It uses an ordered figure-eight descriptor table, bounded repeating contact power and the existing three-body modal exchange. Nonzero NUDGE is refused.

Seventy-five Python checks pass, including true preceding-cycle seams, stored-state recurrence, pitch, source determinism and passive energy decay. Production Kotlin HOLD and held MPC export remain later work. This desktop prototype takes seconds to render; phone performance has not been measured.
