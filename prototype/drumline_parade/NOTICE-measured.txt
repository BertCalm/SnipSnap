Cymbal synthesis reuses BREAKER's measured modes and stick spectra through
CALABASH/EMBER's existing Python helpers. See prototype/ember/NOTICE.txt and
scripts/fit_breaker_cymbals.py. Choke/touch are derived damping gestures, not
fits to recorded chokes/touches.

Rimshot and cross-stick reference WAVs already live in the repository under
synth/src/main/resources/spore-station/v7, under the same CC0 source and its
NOTICE.txt/provenance.json. Hard/soft choices and exact hashes are recorded in
measured-contacts.json. The new coefficients are fitted by SnipSnap's CALABASH
fitter: frequencies, two decay slopes, velocity spectral differences and
residual noise envelopes. No PCM or phases are stored in that fitted file.

The library labels this articulation "stick"; exact free-stick geometry is
not independently verified. Only one velocity level is available here, so its
soft-contact spectrum is a proposed contact-width law, not a measured layer.

The audition separately includes CC0 recorded reference clips for comparison,
trimmed/aligned and level matched. Synthesized pad grids contain fresh modes
and band noise, not playback of those recordings. The samba rim variants are
adaptations of the snare fit; they are not measured caixa/repinique recordings.
No endorsement is implied. All Python results remain audition evidence, not
native Kotlin, hardware or listening approval.
