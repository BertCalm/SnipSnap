Changes: decoded to mono 44.1 kHz; for the flat ride, bell, sizzle crash and
open hat, resonances picked from an 8192-point STFT (a frequency, an amplitude
and a decay each), one level correction per third-octave band, the stick's band
levels over the first 40 ms, the soft recording's band levels against the hard
one's, and the residual band levels the resonances leave out; for the closed and
pedalled hats, each third-octave band's level over time, hard and soft. These
are derived synthesis parameters, not recordings or PCM. The engine synthesizes
sinusoids and band noise; it does not play back any recording, and none ships.
Fitted by prototype/ember/fit_ember_cymbals.py with BREAKER's fitter
(scripts/fit_breaker_cymbals.py). No endorsement is implied.
