THE IDEA

A struck, breathing resonator.

A brief excitation enters six delay lines. The routing changes how energy moves through them. Slow oscillators add breathing motion; a separate glow layer adds harmonics. Revision 2 is another listening test of that idea.

An audition, not a finished engine.
