NEXT / MPC HARDWARE TRIAL

Put the bodies under your fingers.

Four keygroup programs bring SELF, TREFOIL, MIRROR and TORUS to the MPC, with MPC 3 tracks and matching XPM programs. Each uses three sampled references at MIDI 36, 48 and 60, with a playable range of MIDI 30 to 66. PULSE and GLOW are off.

  1. Unzip the entire folder. Keep the extracted program and sample folders in their original places.
  2. Load the MPC program. On MPC 3, load the .xty track. On an older MPC, load the matching .xpm inside its _[TrackData] folder. Choose the program, not an individual WAV.
  3. Try the held gesture. Sustain a note through repeated loop returns, then try short taps, notes between the sampled references, chords and note-off. Listen for clicks, abrupt changes or notes that keep going.

The MPC releases these sampled bodies through its sampler envelope. That release differs from the physical network tail in the recordings below. The MIDI notes are geometry references; perceived pitch can follow another resonant family.
