THE LIMIT

Finite sounds, fully rendered.

Individual engine renders are finite, up to eight seconds. Musical examples can run to twelve seconds by scheduling notes. HOLD extends excitation before decay; it does not create an endless, seamless loop. Pitch across the full KNOT range still needs evaluation.

THE FILES

Take the bank with you.

The bank ZIP contains native WAVs and recipes, with no added effects. Musical-example recipes list scheduled notes and source patches. Each clip also offers its matched audition WAV and measured output. Note labels identify the played reference note; the perceived pitch can differ.
