What changed & source credits

The rejected synthesized sources have been replaced with CC0 acoustic recordings from Virtuosity Drums / Versilian Studios, performed by Austin McMahon. Kick and hats use three recorded round robins at three recorded dynamics. Snare alternates three adjacent recorded strengths within each authored velocity band.

Both treatments use mono conversion, low-frequency cleanup and fixed instrument gain. Open Road adds broad high-frequency softening and gentle kick/snare tail damping. The full grids are energy matched per instrument; individual hits are not normalized. No commercial song audio is sampled.

This is a core listening study. The five-kit family and native SnipSnap gates remain in progress.
