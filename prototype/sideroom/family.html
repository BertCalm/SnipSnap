About the five kits

All four approved kits keep their synthesized pad files unchanged. Deep Pocket uses center, off-center and rimshot descriptors across its three snare velocity levels, guided by Get on Top. High Voltage uses separate off-center snare descriptors, lower shell tuning and longer decays, guided by the I Believe in a Thing Called Love reference. Angular adds a separate rimshot analysis, a lower kick, raised snare/tom resonances and shorter metal decays, guided by the Fashion reference. Tight Pocket uses a separate muted-snare analysis, altered resonant tuning, shorter body envelopes and tighter cymbal decay. It is aimed at the Train in Vain reference, not a claim to recreate the original recording equipment.

All five kits generate noise and oscillator phases from measured spectral and decay parameters. There is no source-waveform playback in synthesis. Three velocity profiles and three generated alternate strikes are included for all eight instruments.

Analysis sources: Virtuosity Drums / Versilian Studios, performed by Austin McMahon, CC0 1.0. No commercial song audio is sampled. Deep Pocket awaits listening approval. Native SnipSnap integration and the remaining acceptance gates are still in progress.
