About the three kits

Open Road and Tight Pocket keep their approved synthesized pad files unchanged. Angular adds a separate rimshot analysis, a lower kick, raised snare/tom resonances and shorter metal decays, guided by the Fashion reference. Tight Pocket uses a separate muted-snare analysis, altered resonant tuning, shorter body envelopes and tighter cymbal decay. It is aimed at the Train in Vain reference, not a claim to recreate the original recording equipment.

All three kits generate noise and oscillator phases from measured spectral and decay parameters. There is no source-waveform playback in synthesis. Three velocity profiles and three generated alternate strikes are included for all eight instruments.

Analysis sources: Virtuosity Drums / Versilian Studios, performed by Austin McMahon, CC0 1.0. No commercial song audio is sampled. Angular awaits listening approval; High Voltage and Deep Pocket and native SnipSnap integration remain in progress.
