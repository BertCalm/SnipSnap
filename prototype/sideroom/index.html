What is built, and what is still open

This is a Python sound prototype. Ten pads per kit include a finite one-bar roll and two-bar lift. The SFZ files retain all velocity layers and takes. SnipSnap shelf integration, native save/playback/export proof, phone/MPC validation and listening approval are still open. Downloaded SFZ playback depends on the sampler.

Reference songs guide the design; their recordings are not included. Prototype tempi are arrangement choices, not measured song tempi.
