How this synthesis works

The synth generates fresh noise and oscillator phases, shaped by frequency and decay measurements averaged from the approved CC0 acoustic hits. It does not play back those recordings. This is analysis-based spectral synthesis, not a complete physical simulation.

Three measured strike-strength profiles change the timbre and dynamics. Each synthesized alternate changes the noise, resonance phase, small tuning differences and decay timing. These are generated variations; the acoustic comparison retains the recorded alternatives.

The acoustic sources are Virtuosity Drums / Versilian Studios, performed by Austin McMahon, CC0 1.0. No commercial song audio is sampled. This listening study does not complete native SnipSnap or full-family gates.
