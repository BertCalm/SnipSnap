What changed, and what was checked

SPACE controls body-color motion in a mono sound. The revision makes one dark–bright–dark excursion in the first 90 ms, with its bright turn at 45 ms. It returns to a steady body color by 180 ms. The amount of movement grows with SPACE; the original static sound remains at zero.

The original loop tuning, source release and excitation remain in place. This is a finite physical-model prototype. There is no room simulation, stereo widening, HOLD or trained neural model.

Fresh checks cover pitch, release, deterministic rendering, equal RMS comparisons and exact recipe replay. A frozen-motion control distinguishes moving color from a fixed color change. These are numerical checks; listening approval remains yours.
