Groove

Two bars at the kit's tempo, played the way the app plays a pad: velocity picks the soft, medium or hard zone, each hit takes the next of four takes, a hat cuts the hat before it, and a sub cuts the sub before it (the sub pads share a choke group, which is how sliding subs are played).

What to listen for

  1. Does the sub slide read as a note moving? A07 and A08 on TRAP, A06 to A08 on DRILL. If it reads as a wobble rather than a second note, say so and I will slow the glide and start it earlier.
  2. How much drive on the sub? Every sub pad has "Clean", "Driven" and "Driven hard" beside "Play pad". A clean sub is correct and nearly silent on a phone; a driven one is audible and less pure. Tell me which you would keep, and on what you listened.
  3. Does the hat roll roll? TRAP A15 tightens from eighths to sixty-fourths and rises at the end. If the last beat is a buzz rather than hits, say so.
  4. Is the drill room right? DRILL's snare, clap and metal hits are in one dark big room; "Play dry" plays the same strike without it. Too much, too little, too dark?
  5. Do soft strikes sound like the same drum, and do the four takes sound like four hits? "Play all 12 strikes": four soft, four medium, four hard. The sub's takes differ mostly in drive and click.
  6. BACKROOM: A01 to A06 should be exactly the Backroom sound you accepted. Do the ten new pads belong with them, and do the swung hats (A15) nod?
  7. LOWRIDER: is the snare's room drenched enough, or too much? Is the laid-back phrase (A15) behind the beat, or just late?
  8. DUST: is the tape thud raw enough with no compressor? Does the unquantized break (A15) read as feel, or as a mistake? A16 adds the crash on the one.
  9. FLINT: do the clicks (A07 to A11) sound thrown, and does the early slide (A05) land in time at 120?
  10. Are these six kits? If any two read as one kit at two tempos, that is the finding.

How to tell me what you heard

The most useful notes name a kit, a pad and a direction. For example: "Trap A07: slide later, longer" or "Drill A02: less room". "Sounds good, proceed" also works and gets recorded as approval of this baseline.

Everything is mono. The records this sound comes from are wide; this page is not.
