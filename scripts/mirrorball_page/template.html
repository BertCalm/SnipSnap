Groove

Two bars at the kit's tempo, played the way the app plays a pad: velocity picks the soft, medium or hard zone, each hit takes the next of four takes, and a hat cuts the hat before it. "Same groove, dry" plays the same strikes with no chain.

What to listen for

  1. Is the punch where you wanted it? On the dotted pads (each kit's kick and snare, plus one more), "Full Punch" is what you heard in round 0b and "Lighter" goes a step further back.
  2. Does the synthesized tambourine pass? Philly A05. "Recorded tambourine, same chain" plays the recording it was fitted to. "Play all 12 strikes" shows the rattle changing every take.
  3. Does each kit sound like disco? Start with the groove, then "Same groove, dry".
  4. How much echo? "No echo" and "More echo" sit either side of what I built.
  5. Do soft strikes sound like the same drum? "Play all 12 strikes" plays four soft, four medium and four hard. A softer hit drives the tape, compressor and amp less, so it should sound more open.
  6. Are these three kits or one? Different drums, tunings, pad roles, tempos, tape and amp.

How to tell me what you heard

The most useful notes name a kit, a pad and a direction. For example: "Philly A02: brighter, less echo" or "Munich A06: pushed amp is better". "Sounds good, proceed" also works, and I'll record it as approval of this baseline.

Everything is mono. Echo times follow each kit's tempo, so the repeats land on the grid only at that tempo.
