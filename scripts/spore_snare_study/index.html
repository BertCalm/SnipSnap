What changed, and what the measurements do not prove

The previous prototype put nearly all its initial energy into low body resonances. This study fits a small bank of oscillators, noise bands and analytic decay envelopes to seven live hits, then measures four separate hits from the same clip. It stores parameters, not a recorded waveform.

Broad spectrum and envelope agreement are useful checks, but they cannot certify acoustic realism. The recording also contains other instruments and processing. The rebuilt snare has not passed listening acceptance or native instrument integration.
