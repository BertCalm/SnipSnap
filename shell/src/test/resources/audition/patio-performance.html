What’s included

Each kit has eighteen pads: sixteen core voices, a hand-drum roll on B01 and a cuíca-inspired friction drum on B02. Every pad has three velocity zones and four round-robin takes in each zone. The genre kits are available on SnipSnap’s starter shelf.

For each solo demo, listen to four soft hits, four medium hits, then four hard hits. They are played from the saved kit; the demo keeps the level differences between zones. The cuíca replaces the riser: a woody friction voice with a pressure bend and rasp. Its short and long examples demonstrate the HOLD length control.

The Cumbia kit retains the approved hard first take. Repeat strikes vary contact, excitation or source settings rather than simply detuning the finished sample. Softer strikes change timbre as well as level. Individual percussion voices stay finite; the roll and cuíca have saved length controls.

Download the ZIP and keep the .xtd beside its matching _[TrackData] folder. These are native MPC 3 tracks with cycling velocity zones and an embedded genre groove. They are not MPC 2 XPM programs. Physical MPC loading is not yet verified.

All genre labels describe authored interpretations, not traditional rhythm transcriptions. Playback files are matched per take using the house bass-weighted level meter; the soft-to-hard dynamics inside each take are preserved.
