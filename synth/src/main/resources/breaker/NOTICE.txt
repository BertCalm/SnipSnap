Changes: decoded to mono 44.1 kHz; resonances picked from an 8192-point
STFT (a frequency, an amplitude and a decay each); one level correction per
third-octave band; the stick's band levels over the first 40 ms; the soft
recording's band levels against the hard one's. These are derived synthesis
parameters, not recordings or PCM. The engine synthesizes sinusoids and a
noise burst; it does not play back any recording, and none ships.
Fitted by scripts/fit_breaker_cymbals.py. No endorsement is implied.
