Fired Bronze dry study, model version 2

TIN, TUN, NA, TE and GA spectral profiles are adaptations of recordings by
ajaysm, "Tabla Strokes", Freesound/CompMusic, December 17, 2012.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source pack: https://freesound.org/people/ajaysm/packs/10737/
Individual source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in each profile's reference field.

Changes: publicly available HQ MP3 previews decoded to mono 44.1 kHz;
45 Hz highpass; peak selection; complex partial envelopes at 400 Hz;
six residual-noise RMS envelopes. These are derived synthesis parameters,
not original recordings or original PCM. The generator synthesizes oscillators
and stochastic residuals. It does not play back the reference waveform.
No endorsement by the recording author is implied.

The short reference files do not establish natural decay times. Off-reference
velocity, damping and tuning are authored transformations, not measured strokes.
The source previews are included only in the audition reference folder with
credit. Their inclusion is not evidence of performer approval.

Published kettle frequency data: William A. Sethares, Tuning, Timbre, Spectrum,
Scale, section 10.3.3, Figure 10.6 (second kettle): 595, 904, 2030, 2326 Hz.
https://sethares.engr.wisc.edu/paperspdf/ttss.pdf

Published gong frequency data: M. H. W. Budhiantho and D. Salim (2018),
Acoustic signal of Javanese 56 cm brass kempul,
Journal of Physics: Conference Series 1075 012021, Table 1.
https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/1075/1/012021
Paper license: CC BY 3.0. Only numerical frequencies are transcribed here;
amplitudes, decay constants and the upward pitch trajectory are authored shapes.

Pot shell/cavity and pressure candidates are uncalibrated mechanism studies.
No claim of measured ghatam mode shapes, validated pressure law, or exact
traditional-instrument reproduction is made.
