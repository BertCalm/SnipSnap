THE CLIPS

Each set is one voice on one note, and its label says which. THE PLAIN ONE is the default, and it is the reference: judge every other clip in the set against it. The clips with only a letter are unlabelled on purpose, and their order means nothing. Some of the lettered clips may be exactly the same as another clip.

HOW TO LISTEN

Tap a pad to play a clip. Under each lettered clip, tap how it sounds next to THE PLAIN ONE. The answers stay greyed out until you have played that clip. The button PLAIN, THEN THIS plays THE PLAIN ONE, waits a moment, then plays that clip. Tap a pad or a button again to stop. LOOP plays what is playing over and over. Keep your volume at one setting for the whole page.

SAME AS THE PLAIN if you hear no difference from THE PLAIN ONE.
FULLER if it has more weight to it.
DULLER if it is more muffled or less bright.
TOO MUCH if it is boomy or thick.
DIFFERENT if you hear a change that is none of those.
CAN'T SAY only if you could not listen to it properly, for example you were interrupted.

Below the clips are the questions that use two clips at a time, and the last few questions. The counter at the top says how many required ones are still open, and how many clips have no answer yet. You can save with some left open. Your answers to those questions stay on this phone until you tap SAVE VERDICT at the very bottom of the page.
