THE CLIPS

Each set is one voice on one note, and its label says which. THE PLAIN ONE is the default, BODY .50: judge every other clip against it. WHAT YOU HEARD LAST TIME is BODY 1 as the engine has it now. The clips with only a letter are unlabelled on purpose, and their order means nothing.

HOW TO LISTEN

Tap a pad to play a clip, then tap MORE BOX, SAME or LESS BOX, each against THE PLAIN ONE. Tap MORE BOX only if you hear a wooden box under the note. If you cannot tell the difference, SAME is a real answer. In the notes box for each voice, say which way and what it sounds like, in a few words. Every clip is dry and mono, at one loudness by the engine's own measure; if one still sounds louder or quieter than THE PLAIN ONE, say so. At the end, say whether you listened on a speaker or on headphones.
