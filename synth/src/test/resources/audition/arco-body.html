WHAT YOU SAID

You listened to BODY on both voices and wrote “Body doesn't seem to do anything” and “Body still seems a little light”. At BODY 1 the box was louder than before, and it did not change what you heard. This page tries other ways of making the box, and you pick by ear.

THE CLIPS

Each set is one voice on one note, and its label says which. THE PLAIN ONE is the default, BODY .50, and it is the reference: judge every other clip against it. WHAT YOU HEARD LAST TIME is BODY 1 as the engine has it now. The clips with only a letter are unlabelled on purpose, and their order means nothing. One or two of the lettered clips may be exactly the same as another clip.

Every clip is level with THE PLAIN ONE by the engine's own measure. On a phone speaker some can still sound louder or quieter; if one does, say so in the notes box.

HOW TO LISTEN

Tap a pad to play a clip, then tap one answer under it, each against THE PLAIN ONE. The button PLAIN, THEN THIS plays THE PLAIN ONE, waits a moment, then plays that clip. Tap the pad again, or the button again, to stop.

SAME only if you cannot tell it from THE PLAIN ONE.
DIFFERENT if you hear a change that is not more box (duller, louder, nasal, brighter, anything).
MORE BOX only if you hear a wooden box under the note, more than in THE PLAIN ONE.
LESS BOX if there is less.

In the notes box for each voice, name the letters: which clips, and what you hear. Every clip is dry and mono.

Nobody here has listened to these. Some of these may sound worse than what you heard before. That is what this page is for.
