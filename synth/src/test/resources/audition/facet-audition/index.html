Inside the instrument

One note.
Many ways to catch it.

FACET imagines twelve beams striking a rotating mirrorball. Each beam carries a different shade of the same note. As reflections pass, the harmonic balance shifts and a small bank of resonances rings in response.

Coarse facets make room for broad, overlapping blooms. Finer facets break the motion into a more delicate shimmer. Stop the rotation and the sound holds its particular angle.

A listening prototype for SnipSnap. These are finite BLOOM and SHIMMER renders; sustained HOLD playback and the expanded editor are future work. Listening acceptance is still pending.
