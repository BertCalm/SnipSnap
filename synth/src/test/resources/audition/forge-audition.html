FORGE

A rack section that forms a hit against a die: a low shock, a sweep through the medium, the fast part folded, then a hard stop with a flash of heat. These clips are the real Kotlin section on real pads. Nobody has heard them yet: your ear is the measurement.

CHARGEAdds a 48 Hz shock under the hit, and spreads the click into a short sweep. STANDOFFWhen the shock lands: 0 to 1 ms after the hit. YIELDHow fast the sound must move to fold. Low folds more; 1 folds nothing. ARRESTThe die: 0 touches nothing, 1 is a hard stop at 0.4 of the peak, with grit. DEPTHHow deep the sweep goes: 0 is the short, high one; 1 is the long one from the low end.

The clips play as the rack lands them, so they keep their peaks and their loudness differences. A number like +2.1 dB is how much louder than dry, at the same peak. "Same loudness" levels three clips to check that tone, not volume, is what you like.
