B

BOTTLE's tail

Does it ring, or burn off?

The one question no measurement settles. From head to tail, body share goes 2.31 → 1.25 on REED and 5.72 → 2.13 on KAZOO — both fade. BOTTLE goes 28.5 → 29.4. It gains. Lowering the decay ratio was swept from 0.15 to 5.0 and made it worse, so the ratio is not the lever. Whether this is a defect or the best thing the voice does is your call.

C

BODY

Can you hear it at all?

Phase 1's central failure. BODY was a mean-removed copy of the window, carrying the same harmonic series the burst already carried, and it measured inaudible. It is now a real second sine at a lower ratio on its own envelope.

D

BLOOM

Does depth read as distance now?

The macro used to mean both how far the formant travels and how fast — coupled, so you could not have a long slow sweep. Six of eight reviewers named it independently. It now means distance only, at a fixed 0.45 s sweep.

F

The staircase

Do the notes step, or slide?

A ruling kept the per-note harmonic snap when the spec's own fix would have removed it. FOLLOW 0 parks the formant so it cannot track the note — the steps are widest there. FOLLOW 0.8 is the shipped default, where the formant mostly tracks and the steps are subtler.
