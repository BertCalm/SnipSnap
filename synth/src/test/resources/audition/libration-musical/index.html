Behind the sounds Measurements, recipes & source +

All sounds are actual Kotlin renders through SnipSnap’s 4× internal DSP path, exported as mono 44.1 kHz WAVs. The rotating-frame three-body model supplies the motion. Spectral mapping, note lengths, and materials are musical design choices. There is no added reverb.

Model 3 adds saved finite source lengths and touch articulation. At the default 80% touch and six-second source, it reproduces the accepted model 2 references exactly. The previous control study and both earlier listening rounds remain available.

Each musical score saves full patches, onset frames and one fixed mix gain. Tails overlap as independent notes; they do not exchange energy across notes. The pad pattern is a computer preview of exported samples, not a recording of MPC hardware.

Render timings are measured on the cloud JVM. Phone response remains unmeasured.

This is a finite audition bank. Sustained HOLD and phone integration remain future work. Your listening and an MPC load test are the next acceptance steps.
