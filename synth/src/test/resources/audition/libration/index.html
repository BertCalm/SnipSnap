Behind the listening studyMeasured results, recipes & source +

All audio is rendered by the Kotlin prototype through SnipSnap’s 4× internal DSP path and exported as 44.1 kHz mono WAVs. Sympathetic resonance is part of the source. No reverb is used.

The rotating-frame three-body model supplies trajectories. Musical time, cloud materials, and the mapping to sound are design choices. Plots show measured displacement from each family’s equilibrium.

This is a finite first-listen prototype. Sustained HOLD, the full six-design preset bank, phone controls, and hardware acceptance remain future work. Your listening verdict is still the musical gate.

A general audio classifier may call these long notes LOOP because they exceed its duration threshold. The exported examples play once.
