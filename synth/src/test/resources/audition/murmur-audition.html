Measured evidence & how to listen

Begin with the expanded preset bank: 8 BASS, 4 KEY, 4 PLUCK, 8 LEAD, 6 CHOIR and 6 METAL. The six dry voices were approved after the CHOIR air trim; that approval does not approve the 24 new presets or complete bank. Review their settings, roots, finite or held behavior and names, using Keep, Refine or Unsure and your listening notes.

Compare six voices remains available at model 2, C4, velocity 1 and each voice's own defaults. Before/after full gestures alternate model 1 and model 2 at identical roots and macro settings. Existing notes keep their clip IDs and may describe an older model; before/after cases have separate IDs. Their articulation and duration can differ; the settled-sustain pairs remove the original attack so that note length alone cannot decide the comparison.

Matched level uses the loudest 200 ms window after a gentle low cut, rather than whole-file RMS or equal peaks. Switch to native level when judging excitation energy. In HOLD, repeat the settled sustain through several wraps; a small seam metric supports the listening test.
