What to listen for

Stay with one note.

The new candidate changes timbre within one articulation. Your notes decide whether that motion is useful and whether the result still feels like one instrument.

  1. 01

    Hear the anchor.

    Compare still, evolving and remembered color at the same note. Listen for a steady pitch through the held portion.

  2. 02

    Play softly, then firmly.

    Compare three velocities in each register. Use Raw for dynamics and Matched to hear how excitation changes the color.

  3. 03

    Shape the articulation.

    Compare quick and slower attacks, then short and long releases after the same held interval. Try the pad bank and export your notes.

A revised listening model

These are rendered audio files with simulated press and release times. This page does not follow a live key or pad release. Each render starts with fresh memory; sample transposition also changes its internal clock. The files are not automatic seamless loops.
