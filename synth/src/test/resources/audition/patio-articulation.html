What to listen for

Llamador: a broad palm contact and short, muted head knock. Repicador: a cluster of finger contacts with a stronger upper-head response. Low agogô: a hollow strike with a hand-damped tail. High agogô: a narrow stick attack and beating, inharmonic upper modes that keep ringing.

The previous pass mainly extended the same bodies. This pass changes how each body is excited and which resonances carry the sound. Use “Hear dry sources” to judge those differences before the tape and amp. Both processed columns use the same Cleaner chain.

Only four pads change. The other twelve and all mix levels stay fixed. Solo copies match their loudest 200 ms RMS window, not LUFS. These are authored synthetic interpretations, awaiting your judgment. MPC hardware loading remains unverified.
