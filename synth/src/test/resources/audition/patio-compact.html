What changed & listening notes

Cleaner Chewed keeps the pitch wobble but reduces tape input, amp drive and sag. A broad cut around 310 Hz clears low-mid buildup, and gentle high-frequency filtering softens the edge. Surdo and cajón tails are shorter in the kit. The scraper’s existing −3 dB mix trim stays.

Listening copies match the loudest 200 ms RMS window, not LUFS. Solo pad levels are matched separately; the roll call preserves their mix balance. The groove uses shared processing; kit pads are processed individually, so their interaction differs. MPC hardware loading remains unverified.
