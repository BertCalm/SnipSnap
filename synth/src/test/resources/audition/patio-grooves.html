What to listen for

Sparse: kick, muted supporting drum, closed hats and both scraper directions. Does the scraper sit behind the pulse?

Full: snare, alegre, repicador, shaker and bells join. Can you still pick out the individual voices?

Fill: hand-drum replies and an open-hat lift emerge as the shaker, scraper and bell pattern make room. Does the kit keep its shape through the change?

These are authored audition patterns, not traditional rhythm transcriptions. They trigger the current processed pad samples at fixed kit levels with amplitude accents and hat choking. No new bus processing or sound changes were added.
