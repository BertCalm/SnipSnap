What changed

The hollow bell is about three semitones lower. Its low body is stronger, with a closely spaced pair of resonances adding a short ring. Damping lets that ring emerge and then settle away.

The Cleaner chain, mix levels and other fifteen pads stay fixed. Use “Hear dry sources” to compare before effects. Solo copies match their loudest 200 ms RMS window, not LUFS. MPC hardware loading remains unverified.
