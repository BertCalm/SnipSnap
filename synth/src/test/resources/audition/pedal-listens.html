First, WAH on the short pads a kit is mostly made of: a closed hat, a rim and a snare cut to 60 ms, each as WAH ships today and with a floor that makes the sweep complete one whole wah before the pad ends. Then three questions the brainstorm could not answer without your ears: whether a SWELL already does what a FADE IN pedal would, whether TAPE's wobble already reads as a VIBRATO, and whether a long ECHO recipe is the DELAY pedal, and whether the rack's one-second tail cap is ever heard on it.

The DELAY on long notes sheet you heard has made way for the DELAY gate: ECHO with the HEAD knob, as built. Tap a clip to play it, tap another to switch. The small number on each clip is its length in seconds. Answers save as you tap. You can skip anything.
