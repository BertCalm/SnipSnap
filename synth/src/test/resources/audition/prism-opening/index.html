CHANGE THE STARTING MATERIAL

The aim is a source-led beginning whose upper colour develops. The body opening and previous opening share a 0.8-second transition and the same destination. Compare the first part of each note, then listen through its arrival.

The original engine render is a secondary reference. Notes belong to each selected version, and earlier-round reviews remain separate.

WHAT THESE FILES CONTAIN

Every source is synthesized by an existing SnipSnap engine. These are finite dry renders with no added FX; drive that belongs to the source engine remains part of its sound. No real-world recordings are used.

PRISM uses spectral colour on its own sine carriers and envelope. A source-led opening borrows the lower-harmonic balance; it does not reproduce the source’s attack, noise or performance.
