CHOOSE A PALETTE FIRST

Listen to the originals before judging the transfers. A source can be a keeper even when its imprint needs work. Notes belong to the selected stage, so those two verdicts stay separate.

WHAT THESE FILES CONTAIN

Every original is synthesized by an existing SnipSnap engine. These are finite dry renders with no added FX; drive that belongs to the source engine remains part of its sound. No real-world recordings are used.

The static imprint carries a smoothed, time-averaged spectral colour into PRISM’s 32 sine partials. It does not copy the original attack, noise or performance. Motion moves into that same colour.
