02 / WHAT TO LISTEN FOR

Speech.
Beating.
Belonging.

This prototype needs a listening verdict. A convincing mechanism is a starting point; a useful SnipSnap voice is the goal.

01

Let PIPE speak.

Listen to the wind, the first hint of chiff, and the body finding its steady tone. Compare restrained and expressive gestures.

02

Stay with MUSETTE.

Hear the reeds moving against one another. Does the beating feel alive? Does the bellows gesture change the body?

03

Check the boundaries.

Held renders begin at the settled tone, without an onset. Velocity and control studies expose one change at a time; existing organs provide the reference.
