What to listen for

Warmer body pulls back the upper registers and drive, giving more of the balance to the existing round foundation. It keeps the played note; it adds no lower octave.

Rich Ensemble is the preferred direction, now saved as a named preset. It increases the separation between the banks and their vibrato, giving more movement while the note holds.

All clips include the keyboard attack and repeated sustain. The downloadable instrument uses the same setting, with a release when you lift the key. This study uses dry mono audio. Physical MPC listening remains pending.
