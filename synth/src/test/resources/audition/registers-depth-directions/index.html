What to listen for

Warmer body pulls back the upper registers and drive, giving more of the balance to the existing round foundation. It keeps the played note; it adds no lower octave.

Richer ensemble increases the separation between the banks and their vibrato. Listen for more movement while the note holds.

Both are setting variations of the current voice, presented for listening. All clips include the keyboard attack and repeated sustain. This study uses dry mono audio.
