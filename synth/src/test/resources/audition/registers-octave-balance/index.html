What changes

Current balance is the calmer ensemble from the earlier comparison. It keeps the round foundation prominent, with restrained upper octave ranks.

More octave raises COLOR to bring those upper ranks forward. That control also opens the tone filter and slightly reduces the round foundation’s share, so judge both richness and brightness.

Bank spread, vibrato, drive, keying controls, pitch and velocity match on both sides. All clips include the keyboard attack and repeated sustain at matched listening levels. More octave is a listening candidate.
