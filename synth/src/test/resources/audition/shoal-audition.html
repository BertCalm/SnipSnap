Meet the population.

Two starting points, the same C3 root and full velocity. Let the whole note develop; the movement lives beyond its first attack.

02

Take it apart.

Compare the full model with one mechanism removed. The motion counterfactual freezes the energy-weighted mean population, retaining the same total-energy envelope. Patch settings, pitch and velocity are identical. Matched playback helps keep loudness from deciding the comparison.

A = full model · B = named diagnostic removal. Center motion remains active when local cohesion is removed. The static-mean counterfactual differs from CURRENT 0, which only disables the directed current.

03

One control. Five steps.

0, .25, .50, .75 and 1. Everything else stays at the selected voice's defaults. Each step is an independent render of a complete finite note.

04

When controls meet.

Compare the four corners. Does a stronger current stay gathered? Does STRAY loosen a coordinated group? Can energy travel farther before it fades?

05

Register & effort.

C1, C3 and C6, each played softly and hard. Try raw playback to hear excitation level; switch to matched for changes in color and concentration.

06

Keep the shoal moving.

Repeat these sustain-only loops through several wraps. HOLD captures and joins a moving energy/STRAY trajectory from the same model, then fits the oscillator phases. Listen for clicks, brightness jumps, or a recurring new attack.

Captured periodic motion is the first-proof approximation. The nonlinear population is not claimed to converge naturally. Adjacent audio cycles are synthesized independently for seam measurements.

07

The busy corner.

All five timbral controls at 1; HOLD 0. Does the pitch remain convincing as the population becomes dense, fast, cohesive and luminous?
