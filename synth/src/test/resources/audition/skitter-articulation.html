What this proof lets you judge

Start with fixed gain and compare soft, medium, and hard. Then try cue matching to hear color with the level difference reduced. A useful result keeps its musical root while touch changes the way the voice speaks.

  • LEAD: listen for a short tear at contact, then recovery into the body.
  • HOLLOW: listen for the upper voice to bloom after contact, then settle.
  • Three retriggers: three copies of the same medium note, each rendered from a fresh circuit with the same seed. This is a reset control, not shared memory between notes.
  • Long sustain: finite HOLD .75 with the natural gate and release. It is not a periodic loop.
  • Raw downloads: original mono PCM24 WAVs. Browser gain, cue selection, and any device resampling do not change those files.

Only quiet tails below −80 dBFS may be shortened, with a 20 ms allowance; timing is shared across all four takes. Within-note phrases still exercise history and recovery. Contact traces explain the mechanism; listening decides its usefulness.
