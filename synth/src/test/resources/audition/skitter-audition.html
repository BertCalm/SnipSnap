Hold the boundary up to the light.

The orange Seams button plays four direct repeats of the final held period. Listen for clicks, extra attacks, pitch movement and distracting noise repetition. Enable Repeat for a longer check. The PCM-24 seam is measured against the required < 1e−3 threshold.

Loop forcing is a documented approximation: gesture timing and noise are periodic, with capped preroll and measured convergence. Numeric thresholds, register coverage and a render-cost budget remain provisional; pitch detection can disagree with a complex pitched texture. The first-1.4-second classifier is a heuristic diagnostic: cropping a pitched sound can produce false percussion labels. Full samples longer than 1.5 seconds automatically receive a LOOP label, so the classifier does not prove pitch.
