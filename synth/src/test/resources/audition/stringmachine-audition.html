WHAT YOU ARE LISTENING FOR

You approved ENSEMBLE in PR-E1: three copies of the finished sound, swimming on two slow sines, summed with no dry signal — the back half of a 1970s string machine. PR-E2 turns that into presets. Four of them, two on VELVET's BRASS voice and two on RESIN's, are the sound the section wants to be sitting on, and each one lands with the ENSEMBLE already in its recipe, so one tap on SEND TO PAD makes the pad you hear here.

Every preset is three things and a control. The pad as it lands now is first: the voice through the section, stereo, with its FOLD beside it — the phone plays the fold on the instant loop and, from this change on, the panel plays it too, so the fold is what you will actually hear when you audition the preset. Then the bare voice, mono, which is what the panel played and SEND TO PAD landed until now. Last, the control: the FANFARE or WIDE SECTION through ENSEMBLE sound you already approved, to be answered against. Each fold's caption carries the measured loss, width and pump against its pair.

These are short, decaying pads — about a second, gone by then — not a held section, because a VELVET or RESIN one-shot has a fixed 3 ms attack and always decays. A pad that swells in and holds is the future ARCO engine's job, or a held-pad instrument; it is not what a one-shot can be. Every number in the presets came from measurement, not from listening, so this page is the first time any of them is heard: KEEP, MEH or CUT each clip, and a CUT on a landed clip means the preset is wrong, not the section.
