04 / INSIDE THE WORKSHOP

Continuous traction, carried by the stone.

SING uses a bounded model of rubbing’s regenerative feedback. Pressure lets vibration grow toward a stable level; loss keeps it contained. That smooth trajectory drives the resonant stone. The initial knock happens once; HOLD maintains the rubbing.

This is an averaged friction analogy, with designed material shapes. It keeps the sung pitch steady while leaving the irregular catches and scrapes for GRIND’s later design. No effects are added to these recordings.
