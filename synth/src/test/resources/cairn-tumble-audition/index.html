04 / INSIDE THE WORKSHOP

What makes it a cascade?

TUMBLE has its own contact law. Gravity brings one launched stone back to a bed. Every impact removes energy, reducing the next rebound’s height and flight time. Its short pitched ring follows the measured impact work. The final tiny rebound sticks.

KNOCK and ANSWER keep their shared-mount mechanics. TUMBLE reuses their acoustic bank, output stage and real continuing-cycle seam check. No effects are added to these examples.

Measurements, limits and source
