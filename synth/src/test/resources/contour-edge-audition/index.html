Level policy and measurements

Matched files use gain only, targeting SnipSnap loudness 0.12 with a 0.90 peak ceiling. A peak-limited recording may remain below the loudness target. Raw downloads preserve native synthesis levels. Revised recipes need the updated Contour build. No rack effects are added.

Held recordings contain one settled period, repeated by the browser with a 5 ms entry and stop fade. A range sequence plays each held step for two seconds, with a 150 ms gap. Finite sequences play each complete rendered note, preserving its attack, with the same gap. Start with your listening volume low.

Measurements check the implementation; listening decides whether each step is useful. These recordings do not establish physical Android or MPC playback.
