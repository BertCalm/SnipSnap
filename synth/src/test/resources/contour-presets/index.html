About the recordings and downloads
  • Play note retains the complete finite attack and decay. Play hold contains one exact settled period and repeats until stopped. Captured held instruments omit the original note attack.
  • Matched playback changes scalar gain only, using SnipSnap's loudness target of 0.12 with a 0.90 sample-peak ceiling. This is not a LUFS measurement. Native playback retains the public engine's output level. No rack effects are added.
  • The held EDGE sequence plays each setting for two seconds, with a short gap. A 5 ms browser entry and stop fade applies to held playback only; the downloaded WAVs retain their rendered samples.
  • Recipe JSON contains the original complete preset. Recording JSON contains the exact finite or held settings of the selected WAV. All WAV downloads are mono PCM16 at 44.1 kHz; the exported MPC bank uses PCM24.
  • The nine original recipes retain their previous saved settings. The 27 additions await your listening decision. Independent render checks cover finite samples, output bounds, recipe replay and real held continuations. Physical Android and MPC playback remain unverified.
