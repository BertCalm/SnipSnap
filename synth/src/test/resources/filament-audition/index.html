Filament is a musical model of guided sound. Ordered delays and bend losses divide the excitation between a retained endpoint and a damped halo.

01

Compare the same excitation

Start with the bend and routing collections. Switch between the collected mix and available stems to hear what leaves the strand, and when.

02

Choose your loudness reference

Native uses the engine’s output calibration. Raw keeps a fixed export gain for energy comparisons. Matched adjusts listening level so you can compare tone. Stem exports keep their supplied gain.

03

Inspect the evidence

Every clip has a saved recipe and render diagnostics. Held clips can repeat with the loop switch; seam context is available when the collection includes it.
