About these listening examples +

What you are hearing

OHM is a SnipSnap listening prototype: a plucked foreground string and two companion resonators. MEDDLING changes their activity; COMMUNE changes their participation in a shared resonance. These controls describe the selected render. The page does not synthesize audio live.

Every example has a defined ending. Gather changes the relationship within one rendered clip; it is not a live control. Joined comparisons play their labeled parts once, with short silence between them.

Level and source

Matched playback adjusts the examples for level comparison. Raw playback preserves their relative output levels and playing dynamics. Switching examples or levels pauses playback so you choose when to listen. The five-setting buttons explicitly start the note you choose.
