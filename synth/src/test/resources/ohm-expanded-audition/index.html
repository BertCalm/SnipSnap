What these recordings contain

The six rows isolate bowing, captured excitation, FM elder, actual source-audio grains, captured-buffer freeze and sustained playback. Independent means MEDDLING .65 / COMMUNE 0; shared means MEDDLING .65 / COMMUNE 1.

The captured hit is original authored audio included with the audition. Its prepared PCM is embedded in the relevant recipes. Freeze captures a populated source-audio buffer; it is not an FFT spectral-freeze claim.

The held clips play the arrival once, then only the marked sustain region eight times. Bowed HOLD uses a captured, blended stretch of continuing physical bow. Their final listening fade does not modify the looping instrument material. Separate notes start independent ensembles.

Raw preserves the renderers’ source dynamics with one common PCM headroom gain. Matched levels each OFF/ON part separately; phrases and held studies are leveled once as complete recordings.
