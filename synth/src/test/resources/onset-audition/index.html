Make a sound.

ONSET blends familiar electronic drum models with algorithmic partners. The accepted kick meets a struck membrane; the snare adds an FM body; both hats cross from a square cluster to resonant noise. Pitch, decay and attack stay connected across the blend.

This prototype is a listening decision for a future SnipSnap engine. The rendered WAVs can sit beside your captures in a kit; the experiment is ready to hear here before app integration.
