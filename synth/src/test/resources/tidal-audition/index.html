03 / THE EXPERIMENT

Movement, with memory.

This is a first listening prototype. Its success is a sound you want to play, with a recognizable connection between encounter and afterstructure.

Two populations keep their identity.

A seeded particle model supplies the movement. Stable groups retain their origins as their shape changes, controlling a small pitched synthesis and resonance bank.

Both populations share the requested root. Their contrast comes from spectrum and articulation. TAIL moves listening emphasis toward material that has actually extended.

Listen for more than a fading echo.

Does the passage change the sound’s structure? Can you still hear the original colors in the exposed strands? Do close and distant encounters leave different answers?

The mechanism comparisons test those connections. A quiet tail in a weak encounter is a valid result; the page does not turn an empty branch into a loud one.
