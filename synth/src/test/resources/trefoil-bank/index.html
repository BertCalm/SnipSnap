Engineering evidence & replay

The synthesis model is unchanged from the revised audition. Presets change controls and finite contact lengths; they do not add rack effects. Sample playback is polyphonic, while the preset and performance player uses one recording at a time. Browser pad playback uses the exported sample zones and SnipSnap’s preview velocity curve.

The MPC files are validated by software read-back. Hardware listening and Android build results are recorded separately from the audio checks.
