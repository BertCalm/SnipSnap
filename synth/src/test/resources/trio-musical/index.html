Download the 16-pad MPC kit

MPC 3: keep the .xtd beside its _[TrackData] folder and set the project to 104 BPM for the included four-bar clip. MPC 2: load the .xpm inside that folder. Source Kit includes the original WAVs and editable kit.json.

Listening and rebuild notes

All pads are finite 3.5-second performances. The samples include their changing alliances and replies. They have no loop or choke group. Hardware and phone listening remain acceptance checks; the browser phrase uses SnipSnap’s documented preview gain and velocity law.

The source is a stylized instrument model. Matching uses SnipSnap’s loudest 200 ms level proxy, not integrated LUFS. Source and asset hashes, full patch recipes and every phrase’s score are included in the pack.

Rebuild: ./gradlew :synth:generateTrioMusicalBank
