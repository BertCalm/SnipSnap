# Hardware acceptance kits

Generated by `./gradlew :audio:generateTestKits` from the tested writer —
regenerate rather than editing by hand.

## Install (5 minutes)

1. Download `SnipSnap_Diag_Kit.zip` (and optionally `SnipSnap_Test_Kit.zip`).
2. Unzip and copy the kit **folder** anywhere onto the MPC's SD card / USB
   drive (not inside `Expansions` — anywhere browsable is fine).
3. On the MPC (**MPC One first** — it's the acceptance device): Browser →
   navigate to the folder → load `SnipSnap Diag Kit.xpm`.

## What each kit answers

### SnipSnap Diag Kit — the numbering question

**Pad N plays N beeps**, and pitch rises a semitone per pad.

- Tap **A01**: one beep → instrument numbering is correct (0-based).
- Tap **A01**: *two* beeps → everything is shifted one pad; the writer needs
  `XpmWriter(instrumentBaseIndex = 1)`. One-line fix, please report which.
- The program fails to load at all → note the exact error text on screen.

Sample names carry their intended pad (`A01_1_beeps`), so a shift is also
visible on the MPC's screen.

### SnipSnap Test Kit — is it a playable kit?

Synthesized drums on the conventional layout (kick A01, snare A02, hats
A03/A04). Things to confirm:

- all 16 pads make sound, at sensible levels;
- **A03 chokes A04** (closed hat cuts the open hat) — that's mute group 1
  surviving the trip;
- pads feel like one-shots, no clicks at the tail.

### SnipSnap Thump Kit — do synthesized kits sound right?

Sixteen synthesized pads: THUMP analog-style drums (kick/snare/hats/clap/
toms/cowbell/rim) on the first twelve, TINES FM metal (zap A13, block A14,
chime A15, bell A16) on the top row — two engines, one kit. Hats share choke
group 1. Regenerate with `./gradlew :synth:generateThumpKit`. Things to
confirm: it loads, it slaps, A03 still chokes A04, and the top row rings
clean.

### SnipSnap Melodic Kit — do note-per-pad kits feel playable?

The first musical kit: A01–A12 walk two octaves of A minor pentatonic on
plucked strings (nylon low, kalimba above, a harp on top), A13–A16 are organ
stabs on the root, fourth, fifth and octave. Root bottom-left, ascending —
the SCALE layout from the keys-on-pads plan, testable before any keygroup
work exists. Regenerate with `./gradlew :synth:generateMelodicKit`. Things to
confirm: it loads, runs feel musical (not just triggered), and no note has a
click or an early cutoff.

## Reporting back

Three facts close out the format work: (1) loads or not, (2) beep count on
A01, (3) whether A03 chokes A04. Plus, separately, the Live III two-byte
check described in `../docs/MPC3_FORMAT.md`.
